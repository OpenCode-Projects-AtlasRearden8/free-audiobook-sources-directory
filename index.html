The Freebie Audiobook Directory

Legal free audiobooks, explained like a friend would · an education project by Asdale Dynamics, LLC

Education only. This is a plain-English directory of legal ways to listen free. It is not legal, medical, or financial advice, prices and catalog sizes change, and nothing here tracks you or costs a dime.

Library Apps (Libby & Hoopla)

Your library card is the single best free-audiobook tool you already own. Here is how the two big apps split the work.

Libby (with your library card)  Library Apps (Libby & Hoopla)

Free with a library card (holds queue, ~20 loans at a time depending on library)

Works on: iOS, Android, Fire tablets, any web browser, Apple CarPlay / Android Auto

“Real new-release audiobooks, free, straight through the library card you already own — just expect a waitlist on the hot titles.”

Hoopla (with your library card)  Library Apps (Libby & Hoopla)

Free with a library card (limited monthly borrows, instant checkout, no holds)

Works on: iOS, Android, web browsers, Roku/Apple TV/Android TV, Apple CarPlay / Android Auto

“Libby's no-wait sibling: instant borrows every month, so grab the niche titles here and let Libby handle the bestsellers.”

Free Trials (Worth It or Skippable)

Trials are only free if you remember to cancel. Each one gets an honest friend verdict: worth it, or skippable.

Audible free trial  Free Trials (Worth It or Skippable)

30 days free (typically 1 credit), then a monthly subscription

Works on: iOS, Android, Windows/Mac apps, CarPlay/Android Auto, Fire tablets, Sonos — widest device coverage anywhere

“The trial is genuinely free for 30 days and lands 1 credit, so binge one big audiobook for nothing, then set a reminder to cancel the same day you sign up.”

Friend verdict: Worth it — if you cancel the same day you sign up; skipping is fine because Libby covers most new releases anyway.

Spotify Premium  Free Trials (Worth It or Skippable)

Free tier exists (ads, shuffle); Premium trial available; Premium includes an audiobook-hours allowance

Works on: iOS, Android, web, desktop apps, game consoles, smart speakers — basically everything

“Premium includes a monthly audiobook listening allowance alongside every song ever, so trial it and see if the bundle actually fits your ears.”

Friend verdict: Worth trying — the music bundle means it almost certainly won't go to waste; check current audiobook terms on the signup page.

Everand (formerly Scribd)  Free Trials (Worth It or Skippable)

Free trial available, then a monthly subscription

Works on: iOS, Android, any web browser, offline downloads

“It's Netflix-shaped for books, but with a smaller library than Spotify or Libby — trial only if you can already name three titles you'd finish this month.”

Friend verdict: Skippable — unless you already have a binge list, Libby and Hoopla will fill in fine.

Chirp  Free Trials (Worth It or Skippable)

Free app and accounts; you just buy the deals you want

Works on: iOS, Android, web player, Apple CarPlay / Android Auto

“Not a trial at all: a free app that blasts daily $0.99-$4.99 audiobook deals to your inbox, no subscription ever.”

Friend verdict: Worth keeping — the only entry here with zero strings and zero guilt.
