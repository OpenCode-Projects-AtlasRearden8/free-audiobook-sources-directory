## Library Apps (Libby & Hoopla)

*Your library card is the single best free-audiobook tool you already own.
Here is how the two big apps split the work.*

**How they differ, in one breath:** Libby has the fewer-but-newer loans with a
waitlist; Hoopla has the more-but-older loans with zero waits. Use both.

### [Libby (with your library card)](https://libbyapp.com)
| | |
|---|---|
| Works on | iOS, Android, Fire tablets, any web browser, Apple CarPlay / Android Auto |
| Cost | Free with a library card (holds queue, loan limits depend on your library) |
| Friend review | Real new-release audiobooks, free, straight through the library card you already own — just expect a waitlist on the hot titles. |

**Libby, explained like a friend would:** install it, pick your library, punch in your
card number, and place a hold on the audiobook everyone's talking about. When your
notice comes through, you get roughly two weeks to finish. You're sharing digital
copies with your whole town, so the waitlist is fair — not broken.

### [Hoopla (with your library card)](https://www.hoopladigital.com)
| | |
|---|---|
| Works on | iOS, Android, web browsers, Roku/Apple TV/Android TV, Apple CarPlay / Android Auto |
| Cost | Free with a library card (limited monthly borrows, instant checkout, no holds) |
| Friend review | Libby's no-wait sibling: instant borrows every month, so grab the niche titles here and let Libby handle the bestsellers. |

**Hoopla, explained like a friend would:** no waitlists ever — your library rents
Hoopla copies on demand and you get a fixed number of borrows per month. That makes it
perfect for graphic-novel audiobooks, older series, and 2-a.m. impulse listens.

## Free Trials (Worth It or Skippable)

*Trials are only free if you remember to cancel. Each one gets an honest friend
verdict: worth it, or skippable.*

### [Audible free trial](https://www.audible.com/ep/freetrial)
| | |
|---|---|
| Works on | iOS, Android, Windows/Mac apps, CarPlay/Android Auto, Fire tablets, Sonos — widest device coverage anywhere |

| Friend verdict | **Worth it** — if you cancel on day one; skipping is fine too, because Libby covers most new releases anyway. |

## The 60-Second Cheat Sheet

1. **Have a library card?** Get Libby (new releases, with waits) + Hoopla (no waits,
   monthly borrows). This alone covers most people.
2. **Want classics?** LibriVox, Loyal Books, or Internet Archive — no card needed.
3. **Story time but make it modern?** Night Vale, The Magnus Archives, Escape Pod —
   subscribe in whichever podcast app you already use.
4. **One big popcorn blockbuster this month?** Audible's free trial, cancel on day one.
5. **Deal-hungry, never-subscribe type?** Chirp.

## Repo map

- `data/sources.json` — canonical source list (single source of truth)
- `build.py` — regenerates `index.html` from `data/sources.json` (stdlib only)
- `index.html` — static HTML index page; open it directly in any browser

python3 build.py                # regenerate index.html
